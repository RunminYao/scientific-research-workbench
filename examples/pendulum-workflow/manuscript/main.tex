\title{Verified finite-amplitude pendulum period}
\author{Workflow example}
\begin{document}
\maketitle
For a point mass on a rigid, massless rod without damping, the period at peak angle
\(\theta_0\) is
\begin{equation}
  T=T_0\left(1+\frac{\theta_0^2}{16}+\frac{11\theta_0^4}{3072}
  +O(\theta_0^6)\right),\qquad T_0=2\pi\sqrt{L/g}.
\end{equation}
At \(L=1\,\mathrm{m}\), \(g=9.81\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}\), and
\(\theta_0=0.2\,\mathrm{rad}\), an independent arithmetic--geometric-mean check
finds that the fourth-order expansion has relative error below \(10^{-7}\), while
the numerical quadrature agrees with that reference below \(10^{-11}\).
These tolerances were checked only at the stated amplitude. The expansion is not
claimed to be accurate for large amplitude or near the separatrix
\(\theta_0=\pi\).
